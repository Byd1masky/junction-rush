Junction Rush

A free traffic-control puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Draw the road, dodge the crashes, get every car home.

How to play

  • Every car has a colored gate on the edge of the map.
  • Drag your finger along the road from the car to its matching gate.
  • Keep cars from colliding and do not make them wait too long.
  • Play 30 handcrafted levels across three worlds, or go for a high score in Endless mode.

Features

  • Traffic lights, roadworks, one-way streets, bridges and ambulances.
  • Earn stars on every level and unlock new worlds.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
