FAQ

How do I play?
Drag your finger along the road from a car to the gate of the same color. Avoid collisions and do not leave cars waiting.

My progress disappeared.
Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app or resetting progress in Settings removes it.

Does the game need internet?
No, it works fully offline.
